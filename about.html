01

The practice

Who / where / how much

Function Psi is not an agency with a meeting room and a fruit bowl. It’s me, a laptop, and whoever I rope in when a project needs another brain.

I work from South Africa, usually with people in other time zones, usually on things that don’t have a reference yet. Some of it is a product launch. Some of it is a tool that only needs to exist for one person. The common thread is that the browser is the material, not just the delivery method.

02

Origin

Why the name / why the browser

I got into this the usual way: opening dev tools on sites I liked and refusing to leave until I understood how they moved. That turned into a habit of building the thing instead of describing it. The habit never wore off.

The name comes from two ideas I keep bumping into. A function is input, rule, output. Psi is the symbol psychology uses for the mind, and physics uses for a wavefunction. I liked that one letter could mean a system and a hunch. That is the job.

“The work should feel inevitable after you see it. Not before.”

What I’m not interested in: templates with the serial numbers filed off, motion that exists because the timeline was empty, and websites that make you wait through a showreel before they tell you what they do.

03

Method

Four moves / repeated until it feels right
01

Find the one move.

Before layout, before code: what is the single thing someone should remember? A camera move, a piece of type, a sound, a joke. Everything else supports it.

02

Build the skeleton in the browser.

I prototype with real type, real scroll, and real performance. A static comp can lie about rhythm. A browser cannot.

03

Make the machine behave.

Responsive, keyboard, reduced motion, slow devices, no WebGL. The polish is in the fallbacks as much as the showpiece.

04

Cut the extra.

If a transition does not explain hierarchy or earn a smile, it goes. Restraint is part of the art direction.

Working rule

The last ten percent is mostly deletion. That is also the part people remember.

04

Toolkit

Tools / not personality

The stack is whatever gets the idea into the browser without sanding off the interesting edges. These are the tools I reach for most, and the ones currently on the bench.

Daily HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Git The floor
3D Blender, Three.js, GLSL The depth
Motion GSAP, ScrollTrigger, Lenis The timing
Sound Web Audio, synthesis, reactive systems The pulse
Design Figma, type, art direction The argument
Shipping GitHub Pages, static-first, performance budgets The proof
On the bench

WebGPU, compute shaders, procedural type, and a small pile of ideas that are not ready to be pages yet.

05

Colophon

How this site is made

This site is the portfolio and the proof. It is hand-written HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no page builder, no template, no theme with the name changed. The 3D opening is a real-time Three.js scene. The motion runs on GSAP and ScrollTrigger, with Lenis for the scroll. The type is Bricolage Grotesque, Instrument Serif, and DM Mono.

03 / Protocol

        
What happens after send.

        
No discovery-call funnel. No automated calendar. Just a short exchange to work out whether the idea and the timing are real.

      

      
    
        
  1. 01
    You send the brief.
    Copy it from the console and paste it into a DM on X. Add anything the form could not hold.
    2. 
        
  2. 02
    I read it properly.
    You get a real reply, usually within 48 hours: questions, a straight no, or a next step.
    3. 
        
  3. 03
    We decide.
    Scope, timing, and whether it is a fit. If it is not, I will try to point you somewhere better.
